In-app bug report

The quickest way to report a bug. The in-app tool automatically attaches a screenshot and device information.

  1. Look for the ladybug icon in the lower-right corner of most screens (upper-right on Mac).
  2. Tap it to capture a screenshot and open the report composer.
  3. Add a description and tap Send.

The report arrives in our inbox directly — no GitHub account needed.
