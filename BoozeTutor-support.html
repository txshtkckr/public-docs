Send us an email

For bugs, content corrections, feedback, or anything else, email is the best way to reach us. We aim to reply within a few days.

Email boozetutor@gmail.com

Reporting a bug

Email us with what you were doing and what happened. These help us find it quickly:

  1. The app’s version and build, shown in Settings › About BoozeTutor.
  2. Your device and its iOS, iPadOS or macOS version.
  3. A screenshot, if the problem is on screen.
