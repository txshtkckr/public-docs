# BoozeTutor — Privacy Policy

**Developer:** Chris Fuller  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-01

---

## Summary

BoozeTutor does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## Data Collection

BoozeTutor collects **no data** from users. Specifically:

- No personal information is collected
- No usage analytics or telemetry are gathered
- No crash reports are automatically sent to any server
- No advertising identifiers are used
- No location data is accessed
- No contacts, photos, or other device data are accessed

## On-Device Storage

All study progress, quiz history, flashcard review states, and app settings are stored
on your device using iOS/macOS system storage. This data stays on your device unless you
turn on iCloud sync (below), and is never accessible to the developer or any third party.

## iCloud Sync (optional)

iCloud sync is off unless you turn it on in Settings › iCloud sync. When it is on, your
study progress (completed chapters, flashcard review schedules, quiz results, study streaks,
study levels, and the Activity log) is stored in **your own private iCloud database** and
shared only between your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This storage is
provided by Apple under [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/) and counts
against your iCloud storage. The developer cannot read it and receives nothing from it.

You can stop syncing at any time in Settings: either on one device only, or on all your
devices, which also removes your progress from iCloud. Either way, the progress on each
device stays on that device. App settings (appearance, content language, and so on) are
never synced.

## Third-Party Services

BoozeTutor uses no third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or crash-reporting
SDKs. The developer receives no data from BoozeTutor, including when iCloud sync is on.

Some reference pages display interactive maps using Apple Maps (MapKit), a system
framework provided by Apple. When a map is displayed, your device may load map tile
imagery from Apple’s servers. This request is handled entirely by Apple and is
subject to [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/). The developer
does not receive, store, or have access to any information about these requests.

## Changes to This Policy

If the privacy practices of BoozeTutor ever change, this policy will be updated and
the “Last updated” date above will be revised. Given that BoozeTutor collects no data,
material changes are not anticipated.
