# BoozeTutor Beer curriculum

The course is organised in strands. Each strand groups lessons, and each lesson teaches one or more topics; every topic says what you should be able to do once you’ve finished it.

Every topic has a level. Basic covers the essentials for enjoying and talking about a drink with confidence. Intermediate adds the grounding of a keen enthusiast or someone starting out in the trade. Advanced goes deeper, into the science, the finer points of production and regions in detail. You choose a level for each drink, and its Lessons list shows the topics up to that level.

## 1. How beer is made

### 1.1 How beer is made

From grain to glass: the brewing process

- **1.1.1 Welcome** (Basic): Find your way around the beer course and its three levels.
- **1.1.2 Ale and lager** (Basic): Distinguish ale from lager by its yeast and fermentation temperature.
- **1.1.3 How beer is brewed** (Basic): Describe each stage of brewing, from mashing to fermentation and packaging.

### 1.2 Beer ingredients

Malt, hops, yeast, water & adjuncts

- **1.2.1 Malt** (Basic): Explain what malt gives a beer: sugar, colour and flavour.
- **1.2.2 Hops** (Basic): Explain what hops give a beer and the forms brewers buy them in.
- **1.2.3 Yeast** (Basic): Explain what yeast does in a beer and how ale and lager yeasts differ.
- **1.2.4 Wheat, wild and flocculent yeasts** (Intermediate): Distinguish wheat, wild and highly flocculent yeasts by what they do to a beer.
- **1.2.5 Brewing water** (Intermediate): Explain how a brewing water’s minerals shape a beer’s style.
- **1.2.6 Adjuncts** (Intermediate): Explain why brewers add adjuncts and what they change in a beer.
- **1.2.7 The four ingredients together** (Basic): Summarise what malt, hops, yeast and water each bring to a beer.

### 1.3 Brewing science

Hops, yeast, water and malt in depth

- **1.3.1 Hop chemistry** (Advanced): Explain the chemistry behind hop bitterness and aroma.
- **1.3.2 Yeast biology** (Advanced): Explain how yeast metabolism makes alcohol, esters and other flavours.
- **1.3.3 Water chemistry in practice** (Advanced): Explain how brewers adjust their water for a style.
- **1.3.4 Malting and mashing** (Advanced): Explain what happens to starch and enzymes in malting and mashing.
