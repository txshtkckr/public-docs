# BoozeTutor Alcohol Curriculum

The course is organized in strands. Each strand groups lessons, and each lesson teaches one or more topics; every topic says what you should be able to do once you’ve finished it.

Every topic has a level. Basic covers the essentials for enjoying and talking about a drink with confidence. Intermediate adds the grounding of a keen enthusiast or someone starting out in the trade. Advanced goes deeper, into the science, the finer points of production and regions in detail. You choose a level for each drink, and its Lessons list shows the topics up to that level.

## 1. How we perceive a drink

### 1.1 Sensory science fundamentals

How taste, smell, and flavor perception work across all beverages

- **1.1.1 Taste, smell and mouthfeel** (Basic): Explain how taste, smell and mouthfeel make up flavor, and recognize the faults found in every kind of drink.

## 2. The chemistry of alcohol

### 2.1 The chemistry of ethanol

Understanding the molecule at the heart of all alcoholic beverages

- **2.1.1 The chemistry of ethanol** (Basic): Describe the ethanol molecule, how fermentation makes it, and how strength and acidity are measured.

### 2.2 Types of alcohol

Comparing ethanol, methanol, isopropanol, and butanol

- **2.2.1 Types of alcohol** (Basic): Tell ethanol from methanol and the other alcohols, and explain why distillers make cuts.

### 2.3 Denaturing

Why and how ethanol is made undrinkable for industrial use

- **2.3.1 Denatured and industrial alcohol** (Basic): Explain why and how ethanol is made undrinkable, and where industrial and fuel ethanol are used.

## 3. Alcohol and the body

### 3.1 Metabolism & BAC

How the body processes alcohol from absorption to elimination

- **3.1.1 Metabolism and BAC** (Basic): Explain how the body absorbs and breaks down alcohol, and what sets blood alcohol concentration.

### 3.2 Effects on the body

Short-term and long-term impacts of alcohol consumption

- **3.2.1 Effects on the body** (Basic): Describe the short- and long-term effects of alcohol on the brain and body.

### 3.3 Final review

Review quiz across all alcohol science topics

- **3.3.1 Final review** (Basic): Review everything in the course.
