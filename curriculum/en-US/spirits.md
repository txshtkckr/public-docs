# BoozeTutor Spirits Curriculum

The course is organized in strands. Each strand groups lessons, and each lesson teaches one or more topics; every topic says what you should be able to do once you’ve finished it.

Every topic has a level. Basic covers the essentials for enjoying and talking about a drink with confidence. Intermediate adds the grounding of a keen enthusiast or someone starting out in the trade. Advanced goes deeper, into the science, the finer points of production and regions in detail. You choose a level for each drink, and its Lessons list shows the topics up to that level.

## 1. How spirits are made

### 1.1 How spirits are made

From raw materials to bottle: the production process

- **1.1.1 Welcome** (Basic): Know what the course covers and the basic path from raw material to bottle.
- **1.1.2 How spirits are made** (Basic): Explain how raw materials, fermentation, pot and column stills and maturation shape a spirit’s flavor.

### 1.2 Distillation & maturation science

Stills, congeners, oak and time

- **1.2.1 Distillation and maturation science** (Advanced): Explain reflux and cuts, where congeners come from, the chemistry of oak, and how age is counted.

### 1.3 Spirit tasting & evaluation

Structured evaluation approaches for professional spirit tasting

- **1.3.1 Tasting spirits** (Basic): Taste and compare two spirits with a structured method.

## 2. Clear spirits

### 2.1 Vodka

The world’s largest spirit category

- **2.1.1 Vodka** (Basic): Explain what vodka is, how its raw materials show, and how it is drunk around the world.

### 2.2 Gin

Botanicals, juniper character & gin styles

- **2.2.1 Gin** (Basic): Describe gin’s history, how it is made from neutral spirit, its style families and its botanicals.

## 3. Whisky, rum and brandy

### 3.1 Whisky

Scotch, Irish, American, Japanese & Canadian

- **3.1.1 Whisky** (Basic): Describe how whisky is made and matured, what the main whisky laws require, and compare whiskies in the glass.

### 3.2 Whisky flavor map

Regional character, flavor terminology, and cask influence

- **3.2.1 The whisky flavor map** (Basic): Describe the character of Scotch regions and of American, Irish, Japanese and Canadian whisky.

### 3.3 Rum

White, aged, dark, agricole & cachaça

- **3.3.1 Rum** (Basic): Describe rum’s styles, regions, production, maturation and labeling.

### 3.4 Brandy & fruit spirits

Cognac, Armagnac, Grappa, Pisco & Calvados

- **3.4.1 Brandy and fruit spirits** (Basic): Describe the main brandy styles, from Cognac and Armagnac to grappa, pisco and Calvados.

## 4. Agave spirits and liqueurs

### 4.1 Tequila & mezcal

Agave spirits of Mexico

- **4.1.1 Tequila and mezcal** (Basic): Explain how agave becomes tequila and mezcal, and how they are aged, regulated and labeled.

### 4.2 Liqueurs & aromatized wines

Flavored spirits, liqueurs, vermouth & fortified aperitifs

- **4.2.1 Liqueurs and aromatized wines** (Basic): Explain how liqueurs, vermouth and other aromatized wines are flavored and made.

## 5. Serving spirits

### 5.1 Serving & review

Glassware, responsible service & exam preparation

- **5.1.1 Serving spirits** (Basic): Serve spirits in the right glass, pair them with food, and serve and refuse service responsibly.

### 5.2 Final review

Review quiz across all spirits topics

- **5.2.1 Final review** (Basic): Review everything in the course.
