# BoozeTutor Sake Curriculum

The course is organized in strands. Each strand groups lessons, and each lesson teaches one or more topics; every topic says what you should be able to do once you’ve finished it.

Every topic has a level. Basic covers the essentials for enjoying and talking about a drink with confidence. Intermediate adds the grounding of a keen enthusiast or someone starting out in the trade. Advanced goes deeper, into the science, the finer points of production and regions in detail. You choose a level for each drink, and its Lessons list shows the topics up to that level.

## 1. Rice and brewing

### 1.1 Introduction to sake

What is sake and why is it unique?

- **1.1.1 Welcome to sake** (Basic): Know what sake is, what the course covers and how to pour for others.
- **1.1.2 Sake rice and the label** (Basic): Explain how sake rice differs from table rice, and read the key facts on a sake label.

### 1.2 How sake is made

The production process from rice to bottle

- **1.2.1 How sake is made** (Basic): Describe each stage of brewing, from polishing and kōji to pressing and bottling, and the choices the brewer makes along the way.

### 1.3 Sake science

Kōji, rice, fermentation and the numbers

- **1.3.1 Sake science** (Advanced): Explain the enzymes, rice chemistry, yeasts and numbers behind a brewer’s choices.

## 2. Styles and tasting

### 2.1 Sake grades & styles

Understanding the classification system

- **2.1.1 Grades and styles** (Basic): Name the sake grades and special types, and explain added alcohol and aging.

### 2.2 Tasting & evaluation

Flavor types, sensory vocabulary, and tasting methodology

- **2.2.1 Tasting sake** (Basic): Taste sake methodically and compare styles side by side.

### 2.3 Beyond sake

Japanese liqueurs and modern styles

- **2.3.1 Beyond sake** (Basic): Describe sparkling, aged and low-alcohol sake, and Japanese fruit liqueurs such as umeshu.

## 3. Sake in Japan

### 3.1 Sake history & people

Ancient origins, kuramoto and tōji, and the yeast strains behind modern sake

- **3.1.1 History and people** (Basic): Trace sake from its ancient origins to today, and the brewers and guilds who make it.

### 3.2 Regions of Japan

Key sake-producing prefectures and their styles

- **3.2.1 Regions of Japan** (Basic): Explain how water and place shape sake in Japan’s major brewing regions.

## 4. Serving sake

### 4.1 Serving & food pairing

How to serve, store, and pair sake

- **4.1.1 Serving and pairing** (Basic): Serve sake at the right temperature and in the right glass, pair it with food, and serve it responsibly.

### 4.2 Final review

Review quiz across all sake topics

- **4.2.1 Final review** (Basic): Review everything in the course.
