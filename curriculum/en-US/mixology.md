# BoozeTutor Mixology curriculum

The course is organized in strands. Each strand groups lessons, and each lesson teaches one or more topics; every topic says what you should be able to do once you’ve finished it.

Every topic has a level. Basic covers the essentials, enough to talk about the subject with confidence. Intermediate adds the grounding of a keen enthusiast or someone starting out in the trade. Advanced goes deeper, into the science, the finer points of production, and regions in detail. You choose a level for each course, and its Lessons list shows the topics up to that level.

## 1. Working the bar

### 1.1 Bar equipment

Learn the essential tools every bartender needs — shakers, strainers, measuring tools, and garnishing equipment.

- **1.1.1 Welcome** (Basic): Find your way around the mixology course and its three levels.
- **1.1.2 Mixing tools** (Basic): Name the tools for mixing a drink and what each is for.
- **1.1.3 Straining tools** (Basic): Name the strainers and when to use each.
- **1.1.4 Measuring and serving tools** (Basic): Name the tools for measuring and serving a drink.
- **1.1.5 Garnishing tools** (Intermediate): Name the garnishing tools and what each is for.

### 1.2 Mixing techniques

Master the core mixing methods: shaking, stirring, muddling, building, blending, and layering.

- **1.2.1 Shaking** (Basic): Shake a drink well.
- **1.2.2 Stirring** (Basic): Stir a drink well.
- **1.2.3 Muddling, building and blending** (Basic): Muddle, build and blend a drink.
- **1.2.4 Shake or stir** (Basic): Choose between shaking and stirring a drink.
- **1.2.5 Egg white** (Intermediate): Use egg white for texture with dry and reverse shakes.
- **1.2.6 Advanced techniques** (Advanced): Use the advanced mixing techniques.

### 1.3 Serving techniques

Learn essential serving skills: straining, rimming glasses, chilling, garnish placement, and working with ice.

- **1.3.1 Straining** (Intermediate): Strain a drink, and choose between single and double straining.
- **1.3.2 Glass preparation** (Intermediate): Prepare a glass: chilled, rimmed or rinsed.
- **1.3.3 Ice** (Intermediate): Choose the right ice for a drink.
- **1.3.4 Garnish and presentation** (Intermediate): Garnish and present a drink.
