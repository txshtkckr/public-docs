# BoozeTutor Mixology Curriculum

The course is organized in strands. Each strand groups lessons, and each lesson teaches one or more topics; every topic says what you should be able to do once you’ve finished it.

Every topic has a level. Basic covers the essentials for enjoying and talking about a drink with confidence. Intermediate adds the grounding of a keen enthusiast or someone starting out in the trade. Advanced goes deeper, into the science, the finer points of production and regions in detail. You choose a level for each drink, and its Lessons list shows the topics up to that level.

## 1. Working the bar

### 1.1 Bar equipment

Learn the essential tools every bartender needs — shakers, strainers, measuring tools, and garnishing equipment.

- **1.1.1 Bar equipment** (Basic): Name the tools for mixing, straining, measuring and garnishing, and what each is for.

### 1.2 Mixing techniques

Master the core mixing methods: shaking, stirring, muddling, building, blending, and layering.

- **1.2.1 Mixing techniques** (Basic): Shake, stir, muddle, build and blend, and choose between shaking and stirring.

### 1.3 Serving techniques

Learn essential serving skills: straining, rimming glasses, chilling, garnish placement, and working with ice.

- **1.3.1 Serving techniques** (Basic): Strain, prepare the glass, choose the ice and garnish a drink well.

## 2. Ingredients and recipes

### 2.1 Ingredients

Explore the essential spirits, liqueurs, mixers, syrups, bitters, and garnishes used in cocktail making.

- **2.1.1 Cocktail ingredients** (Basic): Describe the base spirits, modifiers, mixers, syrups, bitters and garnishes behind cocktails, and make simple house ingredients.

### 2.2 Cocktail recipes

Study the world’s most important cocktails — their recipes, history, variations, and the families they belong to.

- **2.2.1 Cocktail recipes and families** (Basic): Explain what makes a cocktail work, and make the classic recipes of each family.

### 2.3 Cocktail tasting & balance

Apply your knowledge by tasting and comparing cocktails — exploring how base spirits transform the same template into entirely different drinks.

- **2.3.1 Tasting and balance** (Basic): Taste a cocktail critically, compare styles, and adjust a drink that is out of balance.

## 3. History and the modern bar

### 3.1 Cocktail history & culture

Explore the rich history of cocktails — from pre-Prohibition origins through tiki culture to the modern craft renaissance.

- **3.1.1 Cocktail history** (Basic): Trace the cocktail from its golden age through Prohibition and tiki to the craft revival and today.

### 3.2 Modern bar lab

Advanced ingredient techniques used at high-end bars: fat washing, barrel aging, tinctures, clarification, carbonation, and more.

- **3.2.1 The modern bar lab** (Advanced): Use kitchen and lab techniques, from fat washing to clarification, and build drinks by the numbers.

### 3.3 Final review

Review quiz across all mixology topics

- **3.3.1 Final review** (Basic): Review everything in the course.
