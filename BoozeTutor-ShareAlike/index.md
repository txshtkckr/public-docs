# Open-Source & ShareAlike Image Files

Some assets used in BoozeTutor are licensed under Creative Commons
ShareAlike terms, which require that they be made available for redistribution
under the same licence. In compliance with this requirement, these images may
be downloaded from the links below.

Unless explicitly noted otherwise, these images have not been modified from their
original versions beyond trivial adjustments to their scale, cropping, encoding format,
and/or encoding quality to reduce their storage requirements for inclusion in mobile
versions of BoozeTutor. Where applicable, it is the modified version that is provided
here, but links are also provided for the original source, where possible.

While I do not possess any particular photographic skills of my own, some of the
images used in BoozeTutor are my own work. Those images are
Copyright © Chris Fuller 2026 and released here under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) terms.
